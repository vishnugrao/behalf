Across 41 beta tenants: 78% completed first meaningful action within a day,
which is well above the 45% bar we set. Week-four retention is 31%, against a
55% bar.

The qualitative read is that people succeed once and then have no reason to
come back before their next reporting cycle. That is a positioning problem, not
an onboarding one.

Caveat worth stating out loud: 41 tenants is a small sample and nine of them
are internal. Do not quote the 78% externally.
