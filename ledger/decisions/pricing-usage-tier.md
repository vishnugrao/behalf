At GA the seat cap is replaced by metered usage. Existing contracts are
grandfathered for twelve months; new contracts land on the metered tier from
day one.

Two design partners were quoted the metered price in writing before finance
signed off on the floor. If the floor moves up, those quotes are wrong and we
owe them a correction — see [northwind-escalation] for the account most exposed.

Open: nobody has confirmed whether the grandfather clause survives a plan
upgrade mid-term. Legal has the question, no answer yet.
